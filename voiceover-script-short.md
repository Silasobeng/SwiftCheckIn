# WeMotiply — Short Voiceover Script (~75–90 seconds)

Every person who walks through your church doors has a story. But on paper, with visitor information scattered and follow-up left to memory, people get overlooked.

WeMotiply brings it all together.

Create your church profile, personalise your check-in screen, and set up your service — with the date, theme, scripture, and announcements.

On Sunday, activate it and open the kiosk. Members check in within seconds. First-time visitors register with a short, welcoming form. And if the internet drops, check-in keeps working offline — syncing the moment you're back.

As people arrive, your dashboard updates live — who's present, who's new, who's missing.

The moment a first-time visitor checks in, WeMotiply sends a personalised welcome message — by email, or SMS when they don't have one.

The care continues automatically. A birthday message on their day. A thoughtful follow-up when someone misses two services in a row. And you can send your own announcements and broadcasts — to everyone, members, visitors, or any group.

Every person is organised in one directory — roles, cell groups, departments, and full attendance history. Giving is recorded by type, with a digital receipt sent automatically.

And your analytics dashboard turns every Sunday into insight — attendance trends, visitor retention, conversion, your congregation's makeup, and who needs a call. Send or export a full report to your leadership team after every service.

WeMotiply. Together, we multiply — and no soul is forgotten.
